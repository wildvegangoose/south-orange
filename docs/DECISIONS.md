# Editorial decisions (from the site owner)

Recorded so future work on this site follows them. Dates are when the decision was made.

## 2026-11-18

1. **Shared township history: show it on the map, not in house search.**
   - The map shows the jurisdiction's boundary *as it was in the selected year* (Newark → Orange Township 1806 →
     Clinton Township 1834/35 → South Orange Township 1861, which included today's Maplewood → the Village of South
     Orange inside it from 1869 → the Montrose Park annexation 1891 → the independent Village from 1904). Historic map
     overlays stay aligned underneath.
   - The map is centred on today's South Orange. Shared-era landmarks (e.g. where the township's town hall stood) may
     appear outside today's boundary for the years they mattered.
   - **House search is limited to today's South Orange.** Never show Maplewood houses, and never list Maplewood residents
     as South Orange notable people.
2. **The town's name follows the slider:** use the name in use in the selected year (see `MW.nameFor` in `js/data.js`).
3. **Gerardo Catena is listed** among notable people (neutral description).
