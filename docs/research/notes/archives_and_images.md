# South Orange, NJ: digital archives and freely reusable historic images

Research dates: 2026-11-17 to 2026-11-18. Everything below was checked live with curl, the MediaWiki/LOC/TNM/OAI APIs, or web search, unless a line says otherwise. "Verified" means I read the license or rights field on the item's own record (for Commons, the file page's machine-readable license via the API `extmetadata`; for LOC, the item JSON `rights_advisory` / `rights` field).

## Does the South Orange Public Library have a digital archive, and what does it hold?

### Takeaway
SOPL does **not** use Digifind-IT. Its digital local-history archive is a **MediaWiki site at https://localhistory.sopl.org/** (1,184 uploaded files, about 235 content pages). Its biggest pipeline asset is about **3,470 scanned residential real-estate listing PDFs (1960–2000), indexed by street**. All PDFs tested are image-only, with no OCR text layer. The wiki states no license, and its file pages carry no rights metadata, so its images are **not verified as reusable**.

### Cited Findings
- **No Digifind archive:** `https://www.digifind-it.com/southorange/`, `/south-orange/` and `/southorangelibrary/` all return HTTP 404 ("File or directory not found"). The Digifind home page (https://www.digifind-it.com/) has no South Orange link. — [digifind-it.com/southorange (404)](https://www.digifind-it.com/southorange/)
- The SOPL home page links its local-history wiki at `https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/Main_Page`. It also has a "Library History" page (https://www.sopl.org/library-history/). — [sopl.org](https://www.sopl.org/)
- Wiki platform: MediaWiki 1.39.17. API: `https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/api.php`. Site statistics: 1,459 pages, 115 "articles", 2,486 edits, **1,184 files**, 12 users. The site rights fields (`rightsinfo` url/text) are **empty**. — [siteinfo API](https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/api.php?action=query&meta=siteinfo&siprop=general|statistics|rightsinfo&format=json)
- File types via `list=allimages` (3 pages, 1,184 files): 1,018 JPEG, 82 PDF, 60 TIFF, 24 PNG. — [allimages API](https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/api.php?action=query&list=allimages&ailimit=500&aiprop=url|size|mime|timestamp&format=json)
- Main page: "Welcome to the South Orange Public Library's local history wiki! This is a collaborative local history website… With a username and password you can contribute stories, photos…" Usernames come by emailing librarian@sopl.org. The page was last edited 10 Dec 2024. — [Main Page](https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/Main_Page)
- **URL patterns (verified):**
  - Page: `https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/{Title}`
  - Raw wikitext: `api.php?action=parse&page={Title}&prop=wikitext&format=json`
  - File: `https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/images/{h}/{hh}/{Filename}` (MediaWiki md5 hash dirs, e.g. `/wiki/images/0/0b/Village_Hall_with_bell_tower_001.jpg`)
  - 1280px thumbnail: `https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/images/thumb/{h}/{hh}/{Filename}/1280px-{Filename}`. Tested: returns 200 image/jpeg, 117 KB. — [example thumb](https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/images/thumb/0/0b/Village_Hall_with_bell_tower_001.jpg/1280px-Village_Hall_with_bell_tower_001.jpg)
- **Reuse terms:** The "SOPL Local History Collection Policy" was approved by the Library Board in February 2012. It says items chosen for digitization "must be in the public domain or be reproduced with permission of the author". Digital items "may be held locally or reside in a digital repository such as the New Jersey Digital Highway". Also: "It is the responsibility of individual users to respect copyright laws, licensing agreements, and other applicable regulations." No CC or other reuse license is granted anywhere. — [SOPL Local History Collection Policy](https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/SOPL_Local_History_Collection_Policy)
- File description pages carry **no description, source, author or license**. I checked 4 files (Lackawanna_Station_combined.jpg, Village_Hall_with_bell_tower_001.jpg, MontroseTrain_c1868.jpg, Street_Map_of_South_Orange_1860_.jpg): empty revision text and empty `extmetadata`. The `General_disclaimer` and `About` project pages do not exist. — [imageinfo API query](https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/api.php?action=query&prop=imageinfo|revisions&rvprop=content&iiprop=url|size|extmetadata&titles=File:Lackawanna_Station_combined.jpg&format=json)
- **Residential real-estate files:** "Residential Real Estate Files for South Orange properties that were on the market between 1960 to 2000. Each document includes an exterior photo of the residence along with descriptions of the interior and sale prices and dates. The files are organized by street name." Five index pages: [Street Names A – E](https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/Street_Names_A_%E2%80%93_E), [F – H](https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/Street_Names_F_%E2%80%93_H), [I – M](https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/Street_Names_I_%E2%80%93_M), [N – S](https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/Street_Names_N_%E2%80%93_S), [T - W](https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/Street_Names_T_-_W). — [Research your home](https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/Research_your_home)
  - I parsed the five index pages' wikitext and found **3,473 PDF links in 180 street folders**: A–E 587, F–H 658, I–M 635, N–S 806, T–W 787. Largest folders: Harding_Dr 91, Ridgewood_Rd_South 82, Vose_Ave 69, Irving_Ave 62, Ward_&_West_Ward_Pl 60. Each page is a single 300 dpi JPEG (about 1,500×1,040 px). **0 fonts, 0 extractable text characters: no OCR layer**, so OCR is required. — [sample PDF](https://localhistory.sopl.org/wiki/Real-Estate/Academy_St/59_Academy_St.0001.pdf)

### Inferences
- For a pipeline, the SOPL wiki's real-estate PDFs are the best counterpart to Maplewood's Digifind real-estate cards. They can be enumerated without crawling HTML: parse the 5 index pages' wikitext from the API, then download each PDF and OCR it (Tesseract). Expect 1–3 page photo-and-text listing sheets, 1960–2000.
- The wiki's postcards from before about 1927 are probably public domain in the US by age. But the wiki gives no publisher, date or rights for any file, so a site should treat them as "rights not stated, ask SOPL" (librarian@sopl.org) rather than as verified free images.
- The policy's mention of the "New Jersey Digital Highway" suggests some SOPL items may once have been deposited there. I did not check this.

### Gaps
- I did not count how many of the 3,473 real-estate links are dead. Only 5 were downloaded, all with HTTP 200.
- No SOPL-hosted newspapers, yearbooks, scrapbooks or city directories were found online. The *South Orange Record* and the *News-Record* are not in the wiki's file list.
- The sopl.org "Library History" page was not fetched.

## Do the Maplewood Memorial Library's Digifind "Oranges" directories cover South Orange (code "SO")?

### Takeaway
**Confirmed.** The 1950 *Oranges Directory* PDF on Maplewood's Digifind is public, OCR-searchable, and full of residents tagged "SO". Page 5 alone yields 537 text lines containing the SO code. Maplewood's Digifind city-directory collection runs from 1892 to 1970 (33 volumes). Its News-Record newspaper archive is in-library only.

### Cited Findings
- City Directories collection (`view.php?id=1`): "City Directories 33 Items". Years: 1892, 1893, 1894, 1895, 1900, 1901, 1908, 1910, 1912, 1914, 1916, 1918, 1920, 1922, 1924, 1928, 1929, 1932, 1934, 1936, 1938, 1940, 1942, 1948, 1950, 1952, 1954, 1957, 1959, 1961, 1963, 1965, 1970. "Digitization of local City Directories was made possible by the Bequest of Milena Pribramska." — [Digifind Maplewood, City Directories](https://www.digifind-it.com/maplewood/views/view.php?id=1)
- Year page `view.php?id=1&yr=1950`: "1950 22 Items" (parts p1–p22). Each part links to the viewer, `https://www.digifind-it.com/IDIViewer/web/viewer.html?file=/maplewood/data/city-directories/1950/1950_p{N}.pdf`. The raw PDF is at `https://www.digifind-it.com/maplewood/data/city-directories/{YEAR}/{YEAR}_p{N}.pdf`. — [1950 year page](https://www.digifind-it.com/maplewood/views/view.php?id=1&yr=1950)
- The 1950 part 5 PDF downloads publicly: HTTP 200, 15.8 MB, 60 pages. It has fonts (FranklinGothic-Demi, CenturySchoolbook-Bold, non-embedded), and `pdftotext` returns 556 KB of text, so **there is an OCR text layer**. The running head reads "ORANGES DIRECTORY— 1950 281". — [1950_p5.pdf](https://www.digifind-it.com/maplewood/data/city-directories/1950/1950_p5.pdf)
- South Orange entries in that file use the town code `SO` at the end, e.g.:
  - "Bellows Howard (Rita) h 441 Redmond rd SO"
  - "— Sadie wid Aaron h 575 Cameron rd SO" (Bendersky)
  - "— Mildred S wid Alfred C h 215 Vose av SO"
  - "Bennett A David tile N Y r 212 Mayhew dr SO"
  - "— Emma H wid Eli h 108 Montrose av W SO"
  The same pages use `EO`, `O`, `WO` and `M` for East Orange, Orange, West Orange and Maplewood. **537 lines** in the extracted text contain a standalone "SO" token (OCR is two-column, so some lines mix two entries). — [1950_p5.pdf](https://www.digifind-it.com/maplewood/data/city-directories/1950/1950_p5.pdf)
- Digifind's News-Record collection (`view.php?id=2`) shows: "The digital archive of Maplewood Memorial Library can only be accessed at the library." — [Digifind Maplewood id=2](https://www.digifind-it.com/maplewood/views/view.php?id=2)
- The NJ State Library's Essex County digitized-newspaper guide lists "News-Record of Maplewood and South Orange (1903 - 2022) — Free Access via the Maplewood Public Library — Incomplete; 1 issue from 1903 and 1 issue from 1904", linking to digifind-it.com/maplewood. — [NJ State Library LibGuide, Essex](https://libguides.njstatelib.org/digitized-newspapers/essex)

### Inferences
- `tools/town.json` can point at the Maplewood Digifind slug with directory code `SO` to get South Orange residents for 1892–1970. The repo's PLAYBOOK already anticipates this ("the Oranges directories… also list South Orange residents with the town code SO").
- Early (1890s) volumes may use different town abbreviations. Before choosing a pattern, check one page per decade.

### Gaps
- I only checked 1950 part 5. I did not test whether pre-1910 volumes use "SO" or "S O" / "So Orange".
- A search snippet said the Maplewood library describes News-Record coverage as "1915 to the present". That page (https://www.maplewoodlibrary.org/research-maplewooddigital-archive) was not fetched, so treat the figure as unverified.

## What other archives hold South Orange material (Seton Hall, SOHPS, Newark PL, NJ State Archives/Library, Internet Archive)?

### Takeaway
**Seton Hall's eRepository** (Digital Commons) is the richest public image source: **277 South Orange postcards** from the 1890s–1950s, plus Seton Hall photographs, yearbooks and *The Setonian*. It is harvestable by OAI-PMH, but the rights statement reserves reproduction rights, so the postcards are **not openly licensed**. SOHPS has no online archive. I found no Newark PL or NJ State Archives South Orange digital collections that I could confirm.

### Cited Findings
- **Seton Hall, Archives & Special Collections, eRepository index:** Ace Alagna Photograph Collection; Digital Exhibits; Graduate and Undergraduate Catalogues; **Historic Postcards of South Orange, N.J.**; Italian-American Newspapers; NJ Catholic Historical Commission documents and newsletters; Seton Family Photographs; **Seton Hall College Scrapbook**; SHU Athletics Photographs; **Seton Hall University Photographs**; **Seton Hall University Yearbooks**; The Catholic Advocate; The Memory Project; **The Setonian**; Time Machines; and others. — [scholarship.shu.edu/archives](https://scholarship.shu.edu/archives/)
- **Historic Postcards of South Orange, N.J.:** "images of buildings, streets, and locations throughout South Orange, NJ from the 1890s through the 1950s… Included are images of Seton Hall University." Sources: "Historic Postcards of Seton Hall and South Orange Collection, ca. 1890-2006, Mss 0027" and "George Velotto Collection of Historic South Orange Postcards, 1905-1951, Mss 0048". The gallery runs to 35 pages. — [so-postcards](https://scholarship.shu.edu/so-postcards/)
- OAI-PMH: `https://scholarship.shu.edu/do/oai/?verb=ListRecords&metadataPrefix=oai_dc&set=publication:so-postcards` returns `completeListSize="277"`, 100 records per page with a resumptionToken. Titles include "Town Hall, South Orange, N.J.", "View of So. Orange from Town Hall Tower", "Lackawanna Station, South Orange N.J", "South Orange Ave. from Valley St.", "Free Public Library, South Orange, N.J.", "Hook and Ladder Co. No.1 South Orange N.J." and many Seton Hall College views. — [OAI ListRecords](https://scholarship.shu.edu/do/oai/?verb=ListRecords&metadataPrefix=oai_dc&set=publication:so-postcards)
- Item URL patterns:
  - Item page: `https://scholarship.shu.edu/so-postcards/{n}`. Example: /10, "219 Valley St., South Orange, N.J. … Suburban Gift Shoppe", Date Unknown, Identifier 270010, File Name shu00270010.jpg.
  - Preview: `https://scholarship.shu.edu/so-postcards/{article}/preview.jpg` (article 1009 = 23 KB JPEG); `…/thumbnail.jpg` also exists.
  - Original: `https://scholarship.shu.edu/context/so-postcards/article/{article}/type/native/viewcontent` (302 to `cgi/viewcontent.cgi?...`).
  — [item 10](https://scholarship.shu.edu/so-postcards/10)
- **Seton Hall rights statement**, on every item: "All materials available in this collection (unless otherwise noted) are the property of the Monsignor William Noé Field Archives & Special Collections Center and Seton Hall University, which reserves the right to limit access to or reproduction of these materials. Reproduction of materials or content is subject to United States copyright restrictions… to request a high-resolution reproduction contact: Seton Hall University Libraries, Archives & Special Collections Center… 973-761-9476." — [item 10](https://scholarship.shu.edu/so-postcards/10)
- The Seton Hall University Photographs collection covers "Seton Hall University from the end of the 19th century through today… buildings, people, the campus, and events", including many aerial views. Its gallery runs to 37 pages. — [shu-photographs](https://scholarship.shu.edu/shu-photographs/)
- A Seton Hall archives blog post ("South Orange and Seton Hall – Local Research Ties") ties its local research to SOPL and SOHPS. It was seen in search results only and not fetched. — [blogs.shu.edu/archives/?p=2325](https://blogs.shu.edu/archives/?p=2325)
- **SOHPS (South Orange Historical & Preservation Society):** a WordPress site with About, Old Stone House, Events, Join, Links and Donate pages. It meets "the first Wednesday of each month, September through June" and gives an annual Historic Preservation Award (2025: First Presbyterian & Trinity Church). **No online archive or digital collection is linked.** — [sohps.org](https://sohps.org/)
- **Newark Public Library:** The Charles F. Cummings NJ Information Center's digital collections are at npl.org/digital-collections. Its ArchivesSpace (newarkpubliclibrary.libraryhost.com) has records that list South Orange as a subject. A search summary reported "an atlas of Irvington, South Orange, Maplewood and Millburn, 1928" in its listings. My direct search URL `https://digital.npl.org/islandora/search/"South Orange"` returned **404**. — [npl.org digital collections](https://www.npl.org/digital-collections/); [NPL ArchivesSpace repository 3](https://newarkpubliclibrary.libraryhost.com/repositories/3)
- **Internet Archive:** a title/subject search for "south orange" returns 1,218 items. They are mostly SOMAtv community-TV videos (2009–2024), e.g. "Nostalgia Alley- Postcard Collecting" (2009), plus "The South Orange Public Library presents, Our Stories" (2020). A search for "Directory of the Oranges" / "Orange directory" found **no Oranges city directories** (only an 1885 *Orange pocket directory*). — [IA advancedsearch](https://archive.org/advancedsearch.php?q=title%3A%28%22south+orange%22%29+OR+subject%3A%28%22south+orange%22%29&fl%5B%5D=identifier&fl%5B%5D=title&rows=100&output=json)

### Inferences
- Seton Hall's OAI feed gives clean titles and stable IDs for 277 postcards, which makes it a good metadata index for cross-referencing. Displaying the images would need permission from the Archives.
- For images a site can actually reuse, the dependable sources are LOC, Commons and USGS (next sections), not the local institutions.

### Gaps
- **NJ State Archives:** not researched in depth. A search surfaced its newspaper-holdings PDF (https://www.nj.gov/state/archives/pdf/newspaper.pdf), which was not fetched. No South Orange digital image collection was confirmed.
- A search summary named older South Orange papers (*South Orange Bulletin*, 1870–1906; *South Orange Record and South Orange Home News*, 1942–1948). I did not verify these titles, their holdings, or whether they are digitized.
- I did not check the Seton Hall yearbooks or *The Setonian* for format (PDF/OCR) or year coverage.
- The NJ Digital Highway (named in SOPL policy) was not checked.

## What does the Library of Congress hold (Sanborn maps, HABS, Detroit Publishing, other photos)?

### Takeaway
LOC has **two digitized Sanborn atlases of South Orange Village: 1912 (96 images) and 1912–Dec 1949 (78 images)**. Both are explicitly public domain, with JP2/TIFF/IIIF access. Two later paste-over update records (1921 and 1923) are not digitized. I found **no HABS record and no Detroit Publishing photos** for South Orange. LOC does hold about 40 Gottscho-Schleisner architectural photos of South Orange houses (1940–1963), marked "No known restrictions on publication".

## Which USGS historical topo quads cover South Orange?

### Takeaway
South Orange straddles both the **40°45′N (40.75)** parallel and the **74°15′W (−74.25)** meridian. That puts it on **four 7.5′ quads: Caldwell (NW), Orange (NE), Roselle (SW) and Elizabeth (SE)**. It is not just "Caldwell north, Roselle south". On the old 15′ (1:62,500) series it falls on **Morristown (NW), Paterson (NE), Plainfield (SW) and Staten Island (SE)**. The village center (Village Green, about 40.749, −74.266) is just inside **Roselle / Plainfield**.

### Cited Findings
- The OSM administrative boundary for South Orange (Essex County) has bounding box **lat 40.7357–40.7623, lon −74.2832 to −74.2347**. Nominatim places "Village Green at South Orange" at 40.7490, −74.2656. — [Nominatim](https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org/search?q=South+Orange+Village+Township,+New+Jersey&format=json)
- The TNM API (`datasets=Historical Topographic Maps`, bbox −74.285, 40.735, −74.245, 40.765) returned **98 products**. Quad extents from the API:
  - Caldwell (−74.375, 40.75, −74.25, 40.875)
  - Orange (−74.25, 40.75, −74.125, 40.875)
  - Roselle (−74.375, 40.625, −74.25, 40.75)
  - Elizabeth (−74.25, 40.625, −74.125, 40.75)
  - Morristown 1:62,500 (−74.5, 40.75, −74.25, 41.0)
  - Paterson 1:62,500 (−74.25, 40.75, −74.0, 41.0)
  - Plainfield 1:62,500 (−74.5, 40.5, −74.25, 40.75)
  - Staten Island 1:62,500 (−74.25, 40.5, −74.0, 40.75)
  — [TNM API query](https://tnmaccess.nationalmap.gov/api/v1/products?datasets=Historical%20Topographic%20Maps&bbox=-74.285,40.735,-74.245,40.765&max=100&outputFormat=JSON)
- Editions returned. Five- or six-digit numbers are the TNM scan IDs in the filenames.
  - **1:24,000:**
    - Caldwell: 1947 (254218); 1954 (254219–254224, six variants); 1995 (254225)
    - Orange: 1947 (254673); 1955 (254674–254677); 1995 (254678)
    - Roselle: 1943 (472569); 1947 (254837, 472473); 1955 (254838–254840, 254842); 1995 (254843)
    - Elizabeth: 1947 (254343, 254344); 1955 (254345, 254346); 1967 (254347, 254348, 254350); 1995 (254351)
  - **1:62,500:**
    - Morristown: 1888 (255268); 1894 (255269); 1898 (255270, 255271); 1906 (255272–255276, 472450)
    - Plainfield: 1888 (255320); 1893 (255321, 255323); 1898 (255322, 255324); 1905 (255325–255327, 255329, 255331, 472462); 1921 (255330)
    - Paterson: 1888 (255310); 1892 (472458); 1898 (255300); 1900 (255301); 1903 (255302–255309)
    - Staten Island: 1898 (255388); 1900 (255389–255397, 472491)
  - **Other scales:** 1:125,000 Passaic 1900, 1905 (×4), 1942; 1:100,000 Newark 1984, 1986; 1:250,000 Newark 1944–1964.
  — [TNM API query](https://tnmaccess.nationalmap.gov/api/v1/products?datasets=Historical%20Topographic%20Maps&bbox=-74.285,40.735,-74.245,40.765&max=100&outputFormat=JSON)
- The API `downloadURL` pattern is GeoPDF: `https://prd-tnm.s3.amazonaws.com/StagedProducts/Maps/HistoricalTopo/PDF/NJ/{scale}/NJ_{Quad}_{id}_{year}_{scale}_geo.pdf`, e.g. `…/PDF/NJ/24000/NJ_Roselle_254837_1947_24000_geo.pdf`. The Maplewood repo's `tools/town.json` uses GeoTIFF names for the same scan IDs (e.g. `NJ/NJ_Roselle_254837_1947_24000_geo.tif`), and its playbook warns that "a sheet titled 1955 can be a 1982 revision". In town.json the Staten Island sheets sit under `NY/`, while TNM lists them under `PDF/NJ/62500/`. — [TNM API](https://tnmaccess.nationalmap.gov/api/v1/products?datasets=Historical%20Topographic%20Maps&bbox=-74.285,40.735,-74.245,40.765&max=100&outputFormat=JSON); repo file `/home/user/maplewood/tools/town.json`

### Inferences
- A South Orange topo layer needs the **Orange** 1:24k quad (1947/1955) added to Maplewood's Caldwell/Roselle/Elizabeth mosaic, and the **Paterson** 1:62,500 sheet added to Maplewood's Morristown/Plainfield/Staten Island set. Otherwise the township's northeast corner (east of −74.25, north of 40.75) is blank.
- The eastern part of the township, east of −74.25, falls on Elizabeth (south) and Orange (north). This includes much of the Seton Hall campus area. This is an inference from the bounding box; I did not plot campus coordinates.

### Gaps
- I did not check the ScienceBase "End" (revision) dates for the 1954/1955 variants.

### Takeaway
I verified **13 historic (pre-1970) Commons files**, **9 LOC Gottscho-Schleisner photos (1940–1963)** and **2 public-domain LOC Sanborn atlases**, plus **27 later Commons photos of historic places**, all with licenses read from the record itself.

### Inferences
- For galleries, the free historic core is: the 1916 station photo; the 1908 trolley-loop map and 1914 DL&W grade-elevation drawings (NPGallery, public domain); the 1895 *Sketch of Seton Hall College* (PDF page images can be extracted); the Gottscho-Schleisner house photos; and the Sanborn 1912/1949 sheets.
- The modern PD/CC0 photos by Jim.henderson (2009–2010) and the CC BY / CC BY-SA photos (Kofsky, Klotz, Bmzuckerman) can provide "today" views for Village Hall, the station, Mountain Station, the firehouse, the post office, churches, the Stone House, the Kelly Carriage House and Presidents Hall.
- CC BY-SA files need attribution and share-alike. CC BY needs attribution. The PD and CC0 files need neither, though crediting is polite.
- The best route to classic pre-1970 street views (Village Hall c.1900, the gas lamps, South Orange Ave, Orange Lawn Tennis Club, estates) is to **ask SOPL** about its postcard scans, or to scan physical pre-1927 postcards yourself, rather than relying on open-licensed copies, which barely exist online.

### Gaps
- **Rate limits and failures:**
  - Wikimedia Commons: HTTP 429 on most requests at roughly 1 request per 1–2 s. Batches succeeded only with 20 s spacing and 45–135 s backoff, and Commons finally answered "You are making too many requests to the API". I stopped crawling at that point.
  - LOC: 503 on collection and facet JSON searches; item JSON worked with 2–3 s spacing.
  - LOC rights page (`collections/sanborn-maps/about-this-collection/rights-and-access/`): 403 bot challenge. Rights were taken from the item JSON instead.
  - Newark PL Islandora search URL: 404.
- I found no verified-free historic photos of the Orange Lawn Tennis Club, the gas lamps before 1970, Village Hall before 1970, or South Orange Avenue before 1970.
- The "Old Stone House" (Kofsky: "Stone House by the Stone House Brook, 219 S. Orange Ave.") and "Stone House by the Stone Brook" (Klotz: "built in 1773 by Nathaniel Wheeler") captions are at odds with the SOPL timeline's "1680 The Old Stone House is built". Building dates should be settled from NRHP documents, not from these image captions.
