### Inferences
- The list and the category overlap only partly. A complete page needs both, and the category alone adds 53 people, many of them pre-1951 figures (Perine, Beach, Conrow, Leigh Page, Fanning, Monk Johnson, Howe, Havill, Monroe, Redmond, Lozowick, Freilich).
- Columbia High School is in Maplewood but serves both towns (South Orange–Maplewood School District). Many bios cite CHS together with a separate South Orange residence statement, which is good evidence. CHS attendance alone (e.g., Alina Wheeler) does not show a South Orange residence.
- The list article lags its own linked biographies in several places: missing deaths (Chris Browne 2023, Peter Shapiro 2024, Jeff Van Note 2026), an outdated title (Robert Kirsch "nominee", now a sitting judge) and birth-year mismatches (Stacey Kent, Lonnie Wright, Hallett Johnson). Prefer the biography and flag the conflict.
- Connection strength varies a lot. Best: born *and* raised, or long-term resident with a dated quote (e.g., Tony Smith, Bishop, Rebhorn, Fort). Weakest: death-only or category-only (grouped under "Weak…").

### Gaps
- Several living people have no birth year on Wikipedia (Ken Bloom, Andrew Jacobs, Michele Pawk, Allen Barra, Amira Rasool, Peter Waldor, Brenda Pruden Winnewisser), so none is given here.
- The list's source for Stacey Kent (The Record, 2004) has no URL, and the source for Zach Braff (a TVNZ profile) is a dead link. Their South Orange ties rest on the Wikipedia biographies.
- The South Orange connections of John Davidson, Bradford English and Alina Wheeler could not be confirmed from their article text (category membership only).
- Charles Murray's birthplace is disputed (NJ Boxing Hall of Fame: South Orange; Wikipedia: Rochester, NY). Not resolved.
