Notable people

Painters, governors, poets & point guards

People with ties to South Orange, from the men who chartered and laid out the Village to the present day. Each card says how the person is connected: born here, raised here or lived here. Where a published source gives a pre-1951 address for someone no longer living, you can open that house's story. Living people, and anyone whose time here came after 1950, are listed without addresses. People whose ties are really to Maplewood are on the Maplewood site.

Born before 1850

WR

William Redmond

1804–1874Business & Building

Irish-born New York merchant (Wm. Redmond & Sons) and a founder of the Union Club

Bought the Squire farm in South Orange in 1850 and built a brownstone mansion, Hillside, now the Orange Lawn Tennis Club clubhouse.

1829–1874Business & Building

Developer of Montrose, the Village's first planned estate suburb (1867–74)

Laid out Montrose to attract New York businessmen; Village trustee and president of the horse-car line. His home stood at the summit of Montrose Avenue.
